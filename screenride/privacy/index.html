ScreenRide Player — Privacy Policy

Hen Solutions LLC · Effective November 20, 2026

ScreenRide Player ("ScreenRide") is published by Hen Solutions LLC. This policy describes how ScreenRide handles information. It matches the App Privacy answers filed in App Store Connect for the app: Data Not Collected.

Data We Collect

ScreenRide does not collect any personal data. The app does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, identifiers, usage analytics, diagnostics tied to you, or any other personal information, and it does not track you across apps or websites. ScreenRide has no account or sign-up, and nothing you do in the app is sent to Hen Solutions LLC or to any server we operate.

Your Content

The stream addresses you add, the videos you import from Photos or Files, your browser shortcuts, your settings, and your watch history are stored only on your device, in the app's own local storage. Imported videos are copied into the app on your device only; they are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting an item in the app removes it from the device, and deleting the app removes all of it.

The Browser and Third-Party Sites

ScreenRide includes a web browser. The browser loads only the websites you open or tap through to, and any stream you ask it to play comes from a source you choose. Those websites and streaming sources are operated by third parties, are governed by their own privacy policies, and may collect information under those policies when you visit or sign in to them. Hen Solutions LLC does not control and is not responsible for the practices of third-party sites.

Third Parties

Because ScreenRide collects no personal data, there is no personal data to share with, sell to, or disclose to third parties. ScreenRide contains no third party advertising or analytics trackers.

Children

ScreenRide is a general audience app and does not knowingly collect information from children or from any other user.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date before the change applies to the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: Hen Solutions LLC, Leesville, South Carolina, United States. Reach us through the support contact listed on the ScreenRide Player App Store product page.
