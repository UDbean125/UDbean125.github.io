Pith: Book Summaries — Privacy Policy

Hen Solutions LLC · Effective November 10, 2026

Pith: Book Summaries ("Pith") is published by Hen Solutions LLC. This policy describes how Pith handles information. It matches the App Privacy answers filed in App Store Connect for the app: Data Not Collected.

Data We Collect

Pith does not collect any personal data. The app does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, identifiers, usage analytics, diagnostics tied to you, or any other personal information, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Your Content

Books and summaries you add to Pith are processed to generate and display your summaries inside the app. Your library stays associated with your own use of the app; Hen Solutions LLC does not sell, rent, or share your content with third parties for marketing or advertising.

Third Parties

Because Pith collects no personal data, there is no personal data to share with, sell to, or disclose to third parties. Pith contains no third party advertising or analytics trackers.

Children

Pith is a general audience reading app and does not knowingly collect information from children or from any other user.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date before the change applies to the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: Hen Solutions LLC, Leesville, South Carolina, United States. Reach us through the support contact listed on the Pith App Store product page.
